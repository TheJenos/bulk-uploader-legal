Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Summary

Bulk Uploader runs entirely on your own computer. The author does not operate any server and does not receive, collect, sell or share any of your data.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar, username) to show which account is connected, and permission to post videos you choose to your own account.
  • YouTube / Google: permission to upload and manage your own videos and to read your channel's analytics.
  • Your files: the video, thumbnail and caption files you select for upload.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens, upload history and settings are stored only in a local database on your computer (readable only by your user account). Videos are sent directly from your computer to YouTube or TikTok over HTTPS.

Deleting data

Disconnect an account in the app to delete its token, revoke access in your TikTok or Google account settings at any time, or delete the app's data folder to remove everything.
